SLIDE 02 / 20 • BEHAVIORAL_BIOMETRICS.MD

What is Behavioral Biometrics?

Definition

Behavioral biometrics identifies users based on how they interact with devices rather than physical characteristics.

Unlike static methods (fingerprint scans or facial scans), behavioral telemetry measures micro-rhythms in continuous physical inputs.

Verified Interaction Indicators:

Typing speed
Typing rhythm
Mouse movement
Swipe patterns
Touch pressure
Navigation behavior
SLIDE 03 / 20 • BEHAVIORAL_BIOMETRICS.MD

Why It Is Used

Static Security Gaps

Traditional static security checks can be stolen, phished, or bypassed entirely:

Passwords OTPs MFA

Continuous Session Watch

Instead of a "single-gate" login validation, behavioral biometrics continuously monitors user activity throughout the entire session.

This protects operations even if the intruder bypasses active MFA checks.

What is the Push API?

Real-time Mechanism

Push API is a real-time notification mechanism that cuts polling latency down to zero.

The Paradigm Shift

Instead of the bank requesting information (polling), BioCatch automatically sends fraud alerts when risk changes during a session.

SLIDE 13 / 20 • MULE_DETECTION.MD

What is a Money Mule?

Definition

A money mule is a person who receives and transfers stolen money on behalf of fraudsters.

Participation Roles:

• Knowing or unknowing participants.

• Recruited through social media or fake job offers.

Why Banks Care

Money mules directly enable crime rings by allowing them to:

• Move stolen funds

• Launder money

• Hide criminal activity

SLIDE 16 / 20 • SCAM_DETECTION.MD

Scam Definition & Challenge

Definition & Core Types

A scam occurs when a victim is manipulated into willingly sending money to a fraudster.

Scams include: investment, romance, impersonation, tech support, and government impersonation scams.

The Detection Challenge

Because the customer initiates the payment, traditional fraud systems see correct security parameters:

• Correct password

• Correct OTP

• Correct device

The transaction appears entirely legitimate.

SLIDE 17 / 20 • SCAM_DETECTION.MD

Scam Indicators & Workflow

Anomalous Scam Indicators

• Long hesitation / Repeated corrections
• Abnormal navigation / Copy-paste activity
• Active phone call during payment
• Signs of customer confusion

Example Scam Workflow

Customer Login ➔ New Beneficiary Added ➔ Large Transfer Initiated ➔ Indicators Detected ➔ Risk Score Increased ➔ Push Alert Generated
Goal: Identify signs that the customer is acting under manipulation or coercion.
